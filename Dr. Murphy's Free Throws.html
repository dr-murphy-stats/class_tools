Is Dr. Murphy really an 80% free throw shooter?

Dr. Murphy claims to be an 80% free throw shooter. To prove it, she shoots 100 free throws and makes 70 of them. Is she exaggerating, or could an 80% shooter have a day like this just by chance? Assume the claim is true, simulate an 80% shooter, and see how often a result this low happens.

Step 3 A hypothesis test for one proportion

We don't need thousands of simulated sets to determine if this result is unusual enough to cast doubt on the claim. If the claim is true, and the appropriate conditions are met, the sampling distribution of p-hat is approximately Normal, with a mean and standard deviation determined by the claim (p) and the sample size (n). So, we can use the model to find the z-score of our observed proportion and use the Normal model to find the probability of a result like this one (or more extreme) occurring. This is called a hypothesis test.
