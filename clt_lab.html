Part 3 Compare sample sizes

Each panel shows 5,000 sample means from the population you chose, for a different sample size, all on the same 0 to 100 axis. The dashed line marks μ.

Part 4 Compare populations

Each panel shows 1,000 sample means of size n = 100 (the sample size chosen at the top) from each of the four populations. The small outline in each corner is the population shape. Each panel is zoomed to its own μ ± 4.5 σ/√n. Change n at the top to see how quickly each shape becomes Normal.
